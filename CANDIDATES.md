# Candidates — The Funniest Excuse for Skipping a Meeting

Gate: 1–200 chars, single line, not only whitespace. Judge criteria in stated
order: (1) on-subject — a funny one-line excuse for skipping a pointless
meeting/Zoom; (2) sharp, surprising turn; (3) true to office life;
(4) one tight line, no wasted words; (5) fresh angle, not stock wifi/cat/dentist.
Scored 1–5 per criterion, max 25.

## Why C1 wins

C1 is the only candidate that scores 5 on every criterion. It is built from
concrete office artefacts (last Tuesday's minutes, 47 minutes, zero decisions,
one action item, Dave) — specific numbers and a named person, not a generic
complaint. The turn is double: first the deadpan audit of the minutes, then
"Dave, who left in March" (the action item went to a departed employee), then
the closer "I'm skipping the sequel" reframes the meeting as a bad film.
Nothing needs explaining; every office worker recognises the meeting that
produces nothing and assigns work to someone who is gone. C7 and C8 lose on
freshness: the judge brief explicitly demotes stock wifi and cat excuses.
C2–C6 are solid but each has one flat beat (a merely clever line, or a
punchline that lands once instead of twice).
